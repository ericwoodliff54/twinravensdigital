Schooled: Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

Version 1.1 · Effective November 27, 2026 · Applies to the Schooled app, published by Twin Ravens Digital LLC ("we", "us")

1. In short

Schooled has no accounts, no ads and no trackers. Your tanks, fish, notes and photos stay on your device. Photos and text are sent to our server and to Google's Gemini AI only when you use a feature that needs AI (scanning a tank or fish, testing water, asking Get Schooled, or Dream Tank), and only after you allow it. Small cropped fish photos are shared to improve fish ID unless you turn that off in Settings. Schooled is for people 18 and over. We do not sell your data.

2. What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name or email to use the app. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not collect your location or contacts.

3. What stays on your device

Your tanks, fish, water logs, reminders, equipment, progress photos, chat history and backup files are stored only on your device. If you delete the app or clear its data, they are gone unless you made a backup file yourself. We cannot recover them.

4. What is sent, and when

When you use an AI feature, the app sends the request to our server on Cloudflare, which passes it to Google's Gemini service and returns the answer. What is sent depends on the feature:

  • Tank scan and Fish Check: the photo, the names of fish already in the tank, the tank type and the tank size.
  • Water test: the photo of the test strip or kit.
  • Get Schooled: your question, a summary of the selected tank (its name, type, size, filter, your notes, and the fish and other livestock in it) and an optional photo. Anything you type into a name or note field is included, so please do not put personal details there.
  • Dream Tank: your description, tank size and type, and an optional photo.
  • Care estimates: the species name only. Answers are cached on our server and shared, with no personal data attached.

Our server does not keep these photos or questions after the answer is returned (apart from the optional fish crops in section 5). Google processes them under its own terms for the Gemini API.

To enforce fair-use limits, the app sends a random device code (not linked to your name) and our server keeps a usage counter for it for up to about 40 days. To protect against abuse, our server also keeps a counter for your network (IP) address, but only as a one-way salted hash, never the address itself, for up to about 40 days. We cannot read an address back from it.

5. AI processing and your permission

Fish Check, tank scans, the water test, Get Schooled and Dream Tank are powered by Google's Gemini AI. The first time you use one, Schooled asks whether you allow it. If you choose Allow, the information listed in section 4 for that feature is sent through our server to Google for processing. If you choose Not now, nothing is sent and those features stay off. You can change your choice at any time in Settings under AI features. Turning AI off does not affect the rest of the app.

Photos and text you send may contain personal information, such as people, pets or details visible in the background of a photo, or things you type in notes. Please frame photos on your fish and tank only. Do not send photos of people or anything you would not want processed by an AI service.

Our server and Google may be located in the United States or other countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live.

6. Helping improve fish ID

This sharing is on by default. When you add a fish to a tank, Schooled sends a small cropped photo of just that fish, with the name you confirmed, to a shared collection used to improve fish identification for everyone. You can turn it off at any time in Settings. We never send your whole tank photo, tank names, notes, location or account details. Each crop carries only a one-way hashed device code, so it cannot be traced back to you. Because of that, we cannot find and delete one person's crops on request. Turning it off stops future sharing. Crops already shared are kept for as long as they remain useful for improving fish ID, and we do not sell them. A fish photo can occasionally include something besides the fish, which is another reason to frame photos on your fish only.

7. Third parties

  • Cloudflare hosts our server and storage.
  • Google (Gemini API) processes AI requests.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app files.
  • Wikipedia, Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons and iNaturalist supply stock species photos. These sites see the species name and your network address when the photo loads.
  • Apple and Google handle app downloads and, if we offer subscriptions, billing. We may use RevenueCat to manage subscription status; it receives purchase information, not your tank data.

8. Your choices and deleting data

You can delete everything stored on your device by removing the app or clearing its data. You can turn off AI features and fish photo sharing in Settings. Because we have no accounts and keep no name or email, we usually cannot identify data as yours; if you believe we hold personal information about you, or you want to ask about access, correction or deletion, contact us below and we will respond as required by the law that applies to you. You may also have rights to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Age requirement

Schooled is for people 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete what we can.
