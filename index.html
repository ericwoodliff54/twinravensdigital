Our Origins

Huginn & Muninn:
Thought and Memory

In Old Norse lore, the god Odin possessed two sacred ravens—Huginn (representing thought and active perception) and Muninn (representing memory and stored wisdom). Each dawn, they were dispatched across Midgard to scout the unknown, returning at twilight to whisper vital truths in Odin's ear.

At Twin Ravens Digital LLC, we embody this timeless myth. Our software acts as your vigilant scout—gathering real-time safety feeds, remembering your family's dietary vulnerabilities, and alerting you before harm reaches your home.

🛡️ Technology & Product Principles

01
Privacy-First by Design No accounts, no ads, and we never sell your data. Your family profiles and watchlist are stored on your device. Optional household sync is end-to-end encrypted, so we cannot read it. The privacy policy lists exactly what is sent to our servers.
02
Offline-First Architecture Tools should work where people actually shop, including basement supermarkets and rural stores with a weak signal. Rakka saves a product database on your phone so scans stay fast.
03
Objective Regulatory Grounding Recall alerts come from official federal sources (openFDA and USDA-FSIS). Additive notes cite FDA rules, California AB 418 and EFSA, not internet rumors.
