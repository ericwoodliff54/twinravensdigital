Rakka Support

How can we help?

Answers to common questions about Rakka, the grocery recall and allergen scanner from Twin Ravens Digital LLC. If you do not find what you need, email us and a person will reply.

What is Rakka?+

Rakka is a grocery safety scanner. Scan a barcode or a receipt and it checks the product against FDA and USDA recall notices, the allergens you choose, and a list of food additives and synthetic dyes.

Do I need an account?+

No. Rakka has no accounts, no logins and no ads. Your profiles and watchlist are stored on your device.

What is free and what is Plus?+

The free plan includes one profile with all 12 allergen guards, barcode scanning, the watchlist and recall alerts. Plus adds family profiles, household sync between two devices and additive screening.

How do I delete my data?+

Open Rakka, go to Settings, then Privacy and data, and tap Delete my data. This erases your scans, receipts, profiles, settings and the offline product database from your device, and removes background alert and household sync data from our servers. If you need help, email us and we will help you.

How do I stop the app saving a product database on my phone?+

Go to Settings, then Offline product database, and tap Turn off and free space. Lookups will then use the internet. You can turn it back on at any time.

A product or recall looks wrong. What should I do?+

Please email us the barcode number, a screenshot, and what you expected to see. Recall accuracy matters to us, and we review every report. Always check the printed package and the official recall notice before making a safety decision.

How does household sync work?+

In Settings, create an invite in Household. On the other phone, open Rakka, tap Scan partner's QR code, and point the camera at the QR. Your family profiles and watchlist are encrypted on your phone before they are sent, and we cannot read them.

Is Rakka medical advice?+

No. Rakka is an informational tool. It can miss information or be out of date. Always read the printed label, and talk to your doctor about allergies and health questions.

Still need help?

Email contact@twinravensdigital.com. Include your phone model and, if it is about a product, the barcode number. We usually reply within a few business days.
