Know what's in your cart before it's on your table.

Rakka is the grocery guardian that checks every barcode and receipt against FDA and USDA recalls, your family's allergens, and food additives you want to avoid.

See what Rakka does Support

Three checks in one scan

Point your phone at a barcode or receipt and get a clear answer in seconds.

⚠️

Recall alerts

Checks openFDA and USDA-FSIS recall notices, and keeps a copy on your phone so scans keep working with a weak signal or none at all.

🛡️

Allergen guard

Screens for 12 common allergens: gluten, dairy, peanuts, tree nuts, soy, eggs, shellfish, fish, sesame, corn, sulfites and yeast. One profile is free; Plus adds family profiles.

🧪

Additive screener

Flags 37 synthetic dyes and additives of concern, with clear notes on where each one is restricted, including California AB 418 and EU guidance.

Privacy-first by design

No accounts, no ads

You never sign up, and we never sell your data. Your family profiles and watchlist are stored on your device.

Encrypted household sync

Sharing with a partner is optional. Profiles and watchlists are encrypted on your phone before they leave it, so we cannot read them.

Honest sourcing

Recall alerts come from official federal sources (openFDA and USDA-FSIS), not rumors. The privacy policy lists exactly what is sent to our servers.
