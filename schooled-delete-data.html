Delete your Schooled data

Schooled is an aquarium app published by Twin Ravens Digital LLC. Schooled has no accounts or sign-in, so there is no profile on our side to close. This page explains where your data lives and how to delete it.

Data kept on your phone

Your tanks, fish, photos, water tests, reminders and notes are stored only on your phone. To delete them, open Android Settings, then Apps, then Schooled, then Storage, and choose Clear data, or uninstall the app. We cannot see or restore this data.

Data on our server

  • Photos and questions you send to an AI feature (Fish Check, scans, water strips, Get Schooled, Dream Tank) are sent to Google's Gemini through our server to produce your answer. We do not keep them after the answer is returned.
  • Usage counters: to enforce monthly limits, our server keeps counts tied to a random identifier for your subscription or install, and a one-way scrambled version of your network address. The scrambled network address expires after about 40 days. Monthly counts are deleted after about two months, and a subscription's records are deleted after a year without use. They contain no name, email or photos.
  • Community fish photos (only if you turned this on): a small cropped photo of a single fish with the name you confirmed. These are not tied to your name or any account, so we may not be able to find yours. You can still email us to ask.

Subscription records

Your purchase is handled by Google Play and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, using an anonymous identifier. You can cancel in Google Play at any time. To ask for the anonymous subscription record to be deleted, email us.

How to ask us to delete data

Email contact@twinravensdigital.com with the subject "Schooled data deletion". Tell us which of the items above you want removed. We will reply within 30 days. Because we hold no names or accounts, we may ask for details, such as the date you used the app, to find what you mean.
