Privacy-first by design

No accounts, no ads

You never sign up, and we never sell your data. Your tanks, fish, notes and photos stay on your device.

You choose what is shared

Photos and questions go to our server only when you use an AI feature. Helping improve fish ID with small cropped fish photos is optional and off until you say yes.
