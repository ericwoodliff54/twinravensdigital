How we build

Privacy-first

No accounts, no ads, and we never sell your data. Personal information stays on your device wherever we can, and anything shared between devices is encrypted so we cannot read it. Each app's privacy policy lists exactly what is sent to our servers.

Works where you do

Tools should work where people actually are, including a basement supermarket with a weak signal. Our apps keep what they need on your phone so they stay fast.

Honest sources

We use official public sources and say clearly what our tools can and cannot tell you. They are informational aids, not medical advice.

About Twin Ravens Digital

We are a small independent software studio in Ohio. Our name comes from Huginn and Muninn, Odin's ravens of thought and memory: one to watch the world, one to remember what matters. That is the idea behind our apps, tools that keep watch for you and remember what is important to your family.
